# Reputation discovery evaluation

Research only; no production application changes. Evaluation date: 2026-09-25.

Question: Does the research process return NJ startups worth a closer look on
reputation and ecosystem evidence? This does not test investment returns or
commercial performance.

Freeze the sample before new searches: all three prior picks (Queens Carbon,
Chorah Labs, Cecilia Energy), plus the first two companies in the saved January
2026 NJEDA awardee list: Canyon Magnet Energy and CarbonDots. The additional
companies are selected by list order, not by the strength of later findings.
This is a small convenience sample, with a clean-tech and public-program bias.
The first three are retrospective; the latter two are new company reviews.

Assessment rules:

- NJ headquarters or substantive NJ operations must be attributable to a source.
  Accelerator participation in NJ alone is insufficient.
- Strong lead: at least two distinct external organizations with specific
  documented involvement, at least one substantive relationship (joint research,
  license, development partnership, actual program participation), and at least
  one dated qualifying event reported in the last 24 months. Include primary
  confirmation beyond the startup itself. Do not treat several articles about
  the same event as separate events. Record interested parties as such.
- Watchlist: plausible fit with real evidence, but a material identity, location,
  recency, or relationship gap prevents strong inclusion.
- Insufficient: no support beyond one list entry, generic publicity, or unresolved
  identity within the research budget. This is a verdict on retrieved evidence,
  not a judgment that the business is bad.
- Exclude: verified geographic mismatch or confirmed ineligible company.
- Count program selection, recognition and specific affiliations. Do not give
  extra credit for grant amounts, headcount, funding totals, or founder pedigree.
- Search for contradictory facts and relationship changes. No search hits is not
  proof that there are no concerns. Public-interest growth requires dated,
  comparable observations; otherwise mark it unmeasured.

Budget: up to three searches per company and two discovery/challenge searches,
up to 20 new full pages. Reuse captured primary pages where appropriate. Return
all five decisions; do not replace a weak candidate with an easier success.

Grade the process qualitatively: A = strong sourced shortlist with no material
coverage gaps; B = useful leads with material missing coverage; C = mostly weak
or incomplete leads; D/F = unreliable or unsupported output. Report the count
in each decision category and separate untested capabilities.

This is assistant-executed research and self-review, not a blinded benchmark,
automated end-to-end test, statewide sample, or measured discovery precision.
