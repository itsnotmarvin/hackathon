**Summary** :   Read about Cleantech Open Northeast alumni successes and milestones! As the country moves forward towards clean energy and climate conscious activities, Cleantech Open Northeast will be posting news of our alumni throughout the year. We want to highlight these visionary companies and celebrate the successes they experience as they work towards a better, healthier future for all of us. Alumni startups participating in the Cleantech Open Northeast are especially well-positioned to have real potential for global impact. Read about these companies’ successes and transformative, necessary work below.

    \*This blog will cover the entire year, so come back often to see the latest updates! 

    \*\*The years in parenthesis indicate when the startups participated in the Cleantech Open accelerator
