## Energy Independence (4)

* ### Canyon Magnet

  Canyon Magnet Energy designs and manufactures high-temperature superconducting (HTS) magnets, coils and cables for fusion, quantum computing, medical systems, nano research and AI data center power. Its systems run without scarce liquid helium and ship with built-in quench detection and protection and fully automated cryocooling, delivered from its Newark, NJ line in weeks rather than the industry’s typical year-long waits. CME is post-revenue with $500K+ in purchase orders and $1M+ pending from tier-1 customers, a team out of PPPL, Brookhaven and MagLab holding 280+ publications and 40+ patents, and Navy STTR, DOE SBIR and NJEDA support.

  HQ:
  Newark, New Jersey

  Website:

* ### Nascent Materials

  Nascent Materials manufactures next-generation LFP and LMFP battery cathode materials in the U.S., where more than 90% of cathode supply currently comes from Asia. Its pCAM-free thermo-fusion process skips the precursor step entirely, letting it take locally available, lower-grade feedstock and convert it directly into pure cathode material. The Newark, NJ company runs a fully operational bench production facility, has scaled to multi-hundred-kilogram batches, and has 10+ customer sampling programs underway.

  Battery MaterialsCathode MaterialsLithium Iron PhosphateEnergy StorageDomestic Supply Chain

  HQ:
  Newark, New Jersey

  Website:
  nascentmaterials.com
* ### Project K Energy

  Project K Energy is commercializing potassium-ion batteries for AI hyperscalers and next-generation EV OEMs, eliminating lithium, cobalt, nickel and copper from the cell entirely. Removing these materials eliminates the price and supply-chain exposure that comes with them, and potassium-ion chemistry offers advantages over LFP, NMC, and sodium on fast charging, safety, cost, and low temperature performance. The team has moved the chemistry from TRL <2 to >5 in three years, demonstrated 2,000 cycles on small cells and is now testing >1 Ah cells past 200 cycles, and scaled to multi-layer pouch cells. Project K is backed by $2.6M from the U.S. DOE and $650K from the California Energy Commission.

  Potassium-Ion BatteriesLithium-FreeFast ChargingEnergy StorageAI Data Center PowerEV Batteries

  HQ:
  Pasadena, CA

  Website:
  projectk.energy
* ### Terra Fusion Energy

  Terra Fusion Energy is developing centrifugal mirror fusion, a modular approach the company projects can reach net positive energy on roughly $100M of capital investment. Founded in 2024 on 25+ years of patented research and two university experiments, the most recent funded by more than $5M in ARPA-E grants, it is led by the leading figure in centrifugal mirror technology. Terra Fusion has reached 10 million degrees (1 keV) in stable, peer-reviewed discharges and cleared two of the six accepted milestones toward fusion energy on under $1.5M of funding, with funded collaborations at LLNL and AFRL.

  Fusion EnergyCentrifugal MirrorMagnetic MirrorPlasma PhysicsFirm Clean Power
