## Jeremy Sohn

Non-Executive Director

**Appointment to the Board**  
May 2024

**Experience and expertise**  
Jeremy is a venture investor, entrepreneur and life sciences leader with 25 years’ experience developing companies and driving business model transformation.

He is currently managing partner of P74 Ventures, a fund investing in platform technologies. Since 2013, Jeremy has completed 26 investments, led partnerships with over 100 start-ups and industry-leading technology companies and developed eight transformational technology platforms. Prior to launching P74, he was VP, global head of digital BD&L, partnerships & innovation at Novartis, where he oversaw a pharma-tech and digital health venture fund, a digital BD&L partnership and transactions team, and the Novartis Biome innovation community. Prior to joining Novartis, he was managing director at MPM Capital, where he led its pharma-technology fund.

Early in his career, he co-founded an open-source operating system company, Wasabi Systems, where he was CFO and VP of business development.

**Current external appointments**  
Jeremy is managing partner of P74 Ventures.
