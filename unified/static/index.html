    Data notes

    What this catalog can tell you

    Garden State joins four lines of public evidence without turning them into an investment rating. Each profile keeps the source, quote and date close to the claim.

    01

    Coverage, not quality

    A filled pillar means we found something supportable. It does not mean the company is strong, weak or likely to succeed.

    02

    NJ status needs proof

    “Supported” means a source backs the New Jersey connection. A filing address alone does not prove current operations or startup status.

    03

    Roles stay distinct

    Founders, CEOs, advisors and SEC-related people are shown as reported. We do not quietly turn one role into another.

    04

    Dates mean different things

    Published is when a source appeared. Observed is when the research process retrieved it. Both remain visible.
